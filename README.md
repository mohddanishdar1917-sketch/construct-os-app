# 🏗️ ConstructOS

ConstructOS is an AI-powered Construction Operating System designed to simplify and modernize the way contractors, builders, engineers, and construction companies manage their daily operations.

The platform brings together tender intelligence, AI-assisted document analysis, project management, financial tracking, BOQ analysis, document management, team collaboration, and business insights into one unified workspace.

## 🚀 Vision

To become the all-in-one digital operating system for the construction industry by leveraging Artificial Intelligence, automation, and data-driven decision-making.

## ✨ Core Features

* 🔍 Smart Tender Discovery & Tracking
* 🤖 AI Tender Summarization
* 📄 BOQ Analysis & Cost Estimation
* 📁 Secure Document Vault
* 📅 Project & Deadline Management
* 💰 Finance, GST & Invoice Management
* 👷 Team & Workforce Management
* 📊 Business Analytics Dashboard
* 🔔 Smart Notifications & Reminders
* 💬 AI Construction Assistant

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

* **Frontend:** Streamlit (MVP), React (Future)
* **Backend:** FastAPI
* **Database:** PostgreSQL / SQLite
* **Authentication:** JWT & Role-Based Access Control
* **AI:** Large Language Models (LLMs), OCR & Document Intelligence
* **Visualization:** Plotly
* **Deployment:** Docker & Cloud Infrastructure

## 🎯 Mission

Our mission is to reduce administrative overhead, improve project visibility, and empower construction professionals with intelligent software that saves time, reduces errors, and supports better decision-making throughout the project lifecycle.

ConstructOS is built with scalability, security, and usability in mind, laying the foundation for a next-generation construction management platform.
